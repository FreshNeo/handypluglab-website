2. YouTube Focus Filter

YouTube Focus Filter processes its filtering settings and related page information locally in the user's browser. The extension does not upload users' browsing history, search keywords, or video information to HandyPlugLab servers.

Extension settings or preferences may be stored locally in the browser to provide the extension's functionality. The extension's operation is subject to the permissions and disclosures shown in the Chrome Web Store listing.
