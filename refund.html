Refund Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Thank you for purchasing from HandyPlugLab. We want customers to understand our refund process before making a purchase. This policy applies to digital products sold directly by HandyPlugLab, unless a product-specific policy or checkout terms state otherwise.

1. Digital Products

Our products may include browser extensions, software tools, and other downloadable or digitally delivered products. Because digital products may be accessed or delivered shortly after purchase, refund eligibility depends on the circumstances of the request and applicable consumer protection laws.

2. When You May Request a Refund

You may contact us to request a refund in circumstances such as:

  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • You were charged but did not receive access to the purchased product.
  • The product has a significant technical defect that prevents its advertised core functionality, and reasonable troubleshooting has not resolved the issue.
  • The purchase was unauthorized or made in error, subject to verification and applicable payment-provider procedures.
  • A refund is required by applicable law.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at Youhua.Chan@outlook.com.

Please include:

  • Your order number or transaction reference.
  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • The name of the product purchased.
  • A brief explanation of the issue or reason for your request.

Please do not send full payment card numbers or security codes by email.

4. Review and Processing

We will review refund requests individually and may contact you for additional information. If a refund is approved, it will generally be submitted to the original payment method through the payment provider.

The time required for the refund to appear in your account may depend on your bank, card issuer, and payment provider.

5. Subscriptions and Recurring Payments

If a product is offered as a subscription, cancellation and renewal conditions will be shown at checkout or in the applicable product terms. Cancelling a subscription generally prevents future renewals but does not automatically refund a payment already made, unless required by law or otherwise approved under the applicable refund terms.

6. Third-Party Payment Providers

Payments may be processed by Creem or another designated payment provider. Payment-provider procedures may apply to refunds and disputes. Where relevant, you may also contact the payment provider using the information shown in your transaction receipt.

7. Legal Rights

This policy does not limit any mandatory refund, cancellation, withdrawal, or other consumer rights you may have under applicable law. If local law provides stronger protections, those rights will apply.
